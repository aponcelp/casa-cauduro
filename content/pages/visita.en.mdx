The house where Cauduro lived and created.

The Casa Cauduro in Cuernavaca, Morelos, is the home where the maestro spent his last years and the workshop where he shaped many of his most iconic works.

During your visit you will walk the rooms and gardens, and the studio corners where his works were born.
